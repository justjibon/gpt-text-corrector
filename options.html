GPT Text Corrector

Open-source AI writing assistant
How to use
1. Select text on Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, docs, or another webpage.
2. Click the extension icon.
3. Choose Correct, Professional, Casual, Shorten, Improve, Translate, or write your own instruction.

You can also use Ctrl + Shift + G for instant Correct, or right-click selected text.
